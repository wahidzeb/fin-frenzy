About Fin Frenzy

In Fin Frenzy you are a small, hungry shark in a big ocean. Eat smaller fish to grow, keep your hunger bar from emptying, and dodge jellyfish and sea mines.

Drag anywhere to swim and auto-bite does the eating when you are close. There are five ocean zones with bosses like King Crab and the Giant Squid, plus new sharks and gadgets to save up for.

3 quick tips

  1. Swim through the middle of fish schools, not past them.
  2. Keep an eye on hunger and never let the orange bar run out. Save Boost for escaping red fish.
  3. Try follow-finger mode in Settings if the joystick feels tricky. There is also a left-handed layout.
